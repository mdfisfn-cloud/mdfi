01WHY WOMEN’S EMPOWERMENT

Why women’s
empowerment?

When women have the knowledge, resources and freedom to shape their lives, families and communities grow stronger too.

Women’s empowerment means more than access to services. It means having a say in decisions, the confidence to make choices and a fair opportunity to participate in community life and the economy.
