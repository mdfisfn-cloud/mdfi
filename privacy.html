Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

MDFI Social Foundation respects your privacy. This policy describes the information that may be involved when you visit this informational website or contact us using the details provided here.

Information you provide

The contact form on this website asks for your name, email address, area of interest and message. When you submit it, your browser opens WhatsApp with those details in a pre-filled message to MDFI’s number. The website does not send the form to its own server or store the form entries. You can review or edit the message in WhatsApp before choosing whether to send it. If you do, WhatsApp and its service providers process the information under their own terms and privacy practices. If you choose to call us, your phone number and call details are handled by your telephone and network providers.

Technical information

When a website is visited, the hosting provider may process basic technical data needed to deliver and protect the pages, such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested pages and access times. The website’s hosting settings determine what server logs are kept and for how long.

How information is used

Information shared with us may be used to answer enquiries, communicate about our work, maintain the website and address security or operational issues. We do not sell personal information through this website.

Cookies and third-party services

The website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts; when those fonts are requested, Google may receive technical connection information under its own policies. The WhatsApp link opens a third-party service, which is governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We keep information shared directly with us only for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records or meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information in our care, though no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to contact us through the website. If you have a question or request about personal information you have shared directly with MDFI, please call us or write to the office address below. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

Children’s information

This website is intended to provide general information to the public and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Policy updates and contact

We may revise this policy when the website or our practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
